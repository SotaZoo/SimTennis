# Evaluation

The baseline is a frozen PPO serve-return policy. The combined controller retains
that policy for serve returns and selects a separately trained policy for later
incoming shots. Both policies receive the same 34-dimensional observation and
produce the same four force/torque commands. Serving and recovery are scripted.
The selector uses the current point history; physics and recorded replay are
unchanged.

## Protocol

- Generate practice cases with source seeds 81600–81631 and the two frozen
  opponents supplied in the demo bundle.
- Evaluate all 32 first returns and 34 generated legal later-return cases.
- Compare eight first-to-three contests, seeds 96200–96207, crossing both
  opponents, opening servers and court ends.
- Check routing by assigning the same actor to both branches and comparing all
  actions, poses, contacts and scores against the unwrapped actor.

The cases were first evaluated after policy selection. Repeating this benchmark
is a reproduction of that comparison, not a new held-out experiment.

| Measurement | Baseline | Combined controller |
|---|---:|---:|
| Legal serve returns | 31/32 | 31/32 |
| Legal later returns | 6/34 | 12/34 |
| Third returns, included above | 0/3 | 0/3 |
| First-to-three contests won | 6/8 | 8/8 |

## Scope

All six additional practice successes came from second turns after ad-court
serves generated by the newer opponent. The two deuce-court second-turn families
remained 0/8. No successful practice return was lost. The eight contests and one
training lineage do not establish general competitive strength.

The featured 3–1 contest is a selected example from this evaluation. Its matched
baseline loses 2–3. The bundle also retains a symmetric 2–3 contest with only one
legal return per point. Scoring is first to three points, not a regulation set.

The car-and-racket mechanism has a supported planar base. Physics parameters
have not been calibrated against hardware. The human-arm demonstration uses
scripted joint targets and a fixed support; the torso and legs are inactive.

`demo/evaluation.json` contains the original aggregate results. The evaluation
command writes routing checks, individual cases, contest outcomes and a result
summary. Checkpoint and data hashes are recorded in `demo/manifest.json`.
