The character is a presentation mesh. Its arm bones follow simulated body
transforms; it does not supply a biomechanical model or motion data. The original
mesh, skin, materials and texture pixels are retained. The GLB embeds the source
buffer and textures and corrects two source texture filename suffixes.
[Asset provenance and checksums](character/manifest.json).

Court and racket geometry are generated by this project's presentation code.
